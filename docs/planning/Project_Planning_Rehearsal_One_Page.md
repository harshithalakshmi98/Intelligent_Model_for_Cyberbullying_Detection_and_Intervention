# Project Planning Video - One-Page Rehearsal Script (Simple Version, <= 5:00)

**Target total:** 4:50-4:55  
**Delivery style:** simple wording, calm pace, short pauses.

---

## 0:00-0:15 | Opening

"Hello, I am [Your Name]. This is my project planning presentation for *Intelligent Model for Cyberbullying Detection and Intervention*. In the next five minutes, I will explain the motivation, what I have prepared, the research gap, my plan and risks, and how I will evaluate the project."

---

## 0:15-1:05 | Slide 1 - Problem, Significance, Motivation

"Cyberbullying is not only about bad words. It also involves repeated behaviour and relationships between users.  
Text-only models can miss this context, so they may flag safe messages or miss harmful ones.  
My main research question is: how much do peerness and repetition improve detection compared with text-only models?  
The goal is not only better detection, but also better support for intervention decisions."

---

## 1:05-2:00 | Slide 2 - Preparation and Feasibility

"I have already completed important preparation work.  
The project is organised into clear modules for loading data, preprocessing, feature engineering, and evaluation.  
I implemented the EDA pipeline and confirmed the merged dataset workflow works correctly.  
I also prepared a validation strategy that avoids data leakage by using independent subsets only.  
My method choices are supported by literature: start with a strong baseline, then measure feature contribution step by step."

---

## 2:00-2:50 | Slide 3 - Research Gap and Contribution

"I found three main gaps.  
First, many studies focus on text only and ignore repeated interaction patterns.  
Second, many models are tested on one source only, so real-world generalisation is unclear.  
Third, intervention is often discussed, but not converted into measurable model features.  
My project addresses this by combining text with relational and behavioural features, then testing them through ablation, independent validation, and benchmark datasets."

---

## 2:50-3:50 | Slide 4 - Plan, Gantt, Risks, Mitigation

"The plan follows clear phases: data, features, baseline, full model, ablation, validation, benchmark, and reporting.  
The Gantt chart shows this order and dependency.  
I identified key risks: data leakage, class imbalance, runtime limits, scope creep, and delays.  
Each risk has a mitigation and a backup plan.  
For example, leakage is controlled by strict validation subset selection, and imbalance is handled with stratified splits and class weighting."

---

## 3:50-4:50 | Slide 5 - Evaluation, PLESI, Potential Impact

"I will evaluate the project with clear metrics: precision, recall, F1, ROC-AUC, confusion matrix, and A1-to-A4 ablation comparison.  
Validation and benchmark results will be reported per dataset and overall, to show generalisation clearly.  
I also cover PLESI: professional standards and reproducibility, legal data-use compliance, ethical fairness and privacy, and social impact.  
If this project succeeds, it can help moderators prioritise repeated harmful behaviour more reliably and support safer, context-aware intervention."

---

## 4:50-4:55 | Closing

"Thank you for listening. I welcome your feedback."

---

## Quick rehearsal cues

- Speak in short sentences; pause briefly between points.
- Slow down when saying metric names.
- If you go over time, shorten Slide 4 by one line.
